A gear simulator for World of Warcraft® Classic, built on WoWSims Classic by the WoWSims community.

Quick Sim

Install the WoWSims Exporter addon, type /wse export in game, press Ctrl+C to copy the text and paste it here. You'll get your DPS with full raid and world buffs, which you can change on the Settings tab.

Checked against an independent sim: Arms and Fury Warrior. Other specs run, but their numbers haven't been checked yet.
